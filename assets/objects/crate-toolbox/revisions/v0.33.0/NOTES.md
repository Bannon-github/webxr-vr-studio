# crate-toolbox v0.33.0

L3 packaging/perf UPGRADE on the same `objectId`. No GLB. Not a new layer.

- After v0.32 dropped LOD0 `normalMap`, **procedural LOD0 MeshStandard drops packed ORM** (`roughnessMap` / `metalnessMap`) and becomes **albedo-only** — keep **256² albedo `map`**; do not bind ORM; do not allocate the three LOD0 ORM canvases. Constant wood / brass / steel ORM-midtone roughness/metalness match prior LOD midtone constants (wood/handle **220/255** / **8/255**; brass **95/255** / **230/255**; steel **77.5/255** / **235/255**) so `MeshStandardMaterial` stays lit under the present-path ambient fill. `L2_TEXTURE_SIZE` stays **256**. `L3_LOD_ALBEDO_SIZE` stays **256** as the historical mid/far half-res constant (v0.26–v0.28).
- LOD1 stays v0.30 color-only unlit `MeshBasicMaterial` (wood albedo midtone **`0x633318`**, brass albedo midtone **`0xBE7E31`**, no map). Unchanged this pulse.
- LOD2 stays v0.29 color-only unlit `MeshBasicMaterial` at wood midtone `0x633318` (no map). Unchanged this pulse.
- Unique canvases **6 → 3** (wood / brass / steel albedo only). Fastener is not an LOD mesh; it stays on shared LOD0 brass MeshStandard (now albedo-only, no `normalMap`, no ORM). `setToolboxLod` is still visibility-only (no per-switch material swap, no frame-loop allocation). Draws unchanged: 14 / 8 / 2 + fastener 1. Texture pref **256** / max 1024 (LOD0 albedo); mid and far are color-only.
- Geometry, collider names, and L4/L5 activity are the v0.4.0–v0.32.0 set. Session present-path chain (v0.15–v0.22) and v0.8 allocation scrub stay as they were.
- **KTX2 / Basis** remains the packaging step when a DCC GLB lands (`docs/performance/ktx2-quest3-packaging.md`). Author LOD0 PBR that may be **albedo-only ≤256²** (no `normalTexture`, no ORM texture) with constant roughness/metalness. Author LOD1 and LOD2 as **unlit/basic** (or `KHR_materials_unlit`) **without** a `baseColorTexture` (or a tiny 1×1 / vertex color). The runtime prefers a packaged GLB when present and does **not** rewrite materials at ingest. No GLB in this revision.
- Quest 3 frame time / FFR still **unmeasured**. Do not treat this pulse as a 90 Hz pass. Cheaper LOD0 fragments / less ORM-map bandwidth at arm’s length (canvases 6 → 3) is the intended delta.
